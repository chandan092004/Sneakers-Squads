The Squad Story

More Than Just Footwear — Welcome to Sneaker Squad

Born from a relentless passion for street culture and authentic kicks, Sneaker Squad was founded to bridge the gap between hard-to-find deadstock drops and reliable, transparent online buying.

What started as a close-knit circle of dedicated sneakerheads in 2021 has expanded into India's premier curated marketplace. We believe sneakers aren't just shoes on your feet—they are an extension of your identity, attitude, and creative expression. Every drop we curate passes rigorous squad authentication so you never have to second-guess what arrives at your doorstep.
